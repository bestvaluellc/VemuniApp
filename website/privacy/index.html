Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Best Value LLC (“Best Value,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Vemuni mobile application and this website.

Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in, we receive information needed to identify and maintain your account. Depending on the sign-in method you choose, this may include your email address, name, authentication provider, and a unique account identifier. You may also use supported guest sign-in.

Menu and receipt information

When you request a scan, the menu or receipt images you select are uploaded for analysis. The analysis produces information such as menu text, dish explanations, prices, wine and drink details, receipt line items, totals, tax, service charges, and gratuity indicators.

Vemuni’s analysis endpoint does not save the uploaded source images after processing. Scan results are saved to your account so you can access your scan history. For receipt scans, a processed copy of the receipt image is also saved so you can view it again. Menu source images are not saved to your scan history.

My Picks and saved scans

We store your saved scan results, selected menu items, scan date, restaurant name when detected, page count, and related details so these features work across sessions.

Shared menu links

If you create a menu QR code, Vemuni stores a copy of the extracted menu text and details behind a random share link. The shared copy does not include original menu photos or your account information. Anyone who receives the link can view the shared menu, so share it only with people you intend to invite.

Anonymous Table Picks

People using a shared menu may add anonymous Table Picks. Vemuni stores a random browser identifier and the selected menu-item identifiers so combined pick counts can be shown to the person who shared the menu and other people using that link. Table Picks do not include a guest’s name or contact information and may be cleared by the person who shared the menu.

Usage and diagnostic information

We use limited analytics to understand whether features are working. Events may include opening a scan, choosing a photo source, starting or completing a scan, broad error categories, scan type, result counts, detected-language label, and whether certain feature indicators were present. We do not place menu text, receipt values, uploaded images, or email addresses in Vemuni’s custom analytics events.

How we use information

  • Provide menu and receipt analysis.
  • Authenticate your account and protect the service.
  • Save and retrieve scans and My Picks.
  • Apply scan allowances and prevent abuse.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve Vemuni.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

How information is shared

We use service providers to operate Vemuni. These include Firebase services provided by Google for authentication, cloud storage, databases, and analytics, and OpenAI services for menu and receipt image analysis. If you choose Apple or Google sign-in, that provider processes information according to its own privacy terms.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect the safety and security of users or the service, or as part of a business transaction such as a merger or acquisition. We do not sell your personal information.

Storage and retention

Saved scans and related information remain associated with your account until you delete an individual scan or request deletion of your account and associated data, except where we must retain limited information for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.

Shared-menu links do not automatically expire. Deleting the original saved scan does not currently delete a separately shared menu. You may request deletion of shared-menu information by contacting us. We may retain limited operational records for security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.

Your choices

You may delete individual saved scans from within Vemuni. Deleting a receipt scan also deletes its saved receipt image. To request account deletion, deletion of associated data, or help accessing or correcting your information, email vemuni@bestvaluellc.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Children’s privacy

Vemuni is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can address it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

International processing

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. Those locations may have different data-protection laws than your location.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Vemuni changes. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date.
