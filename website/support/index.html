Frequently asked questions

How do I get the clearest scan?

Hold your phone parallel to the menu, include the full page, avoid glare and shadows, and make sure the text is in focus. For a long menu, scan the pages in their original order.

Why did Vemuni read an item or price incorrectly?

Stylized fonts, glare, folds, low contrast, and unusual layouts can affect image analysis. Try a closer, sharper photo. Always confirm prices and menu details with the restaurant before ordering.

Can I search a scanned menu?

Yes. Search checks item names, descriptions, ingredients, categories, and available wine-pairing details. For example, a search for “steak” may also show wines commonly paired with steak.

Where are My Picks saved?

My Picks are saved with that menu in your Vemuni account. Open Saved Scans and select the menu to see them again.

Does Vemuni guarantee allergen information?

No. Allergen and dietary indicators are cautious informational hints and may be incomplete or incorrect. Recipes and kitchen practices can change. Always speak directly with restaurant staff about allergies and dietary needs.

How does bill checking work?

Scan a restaurant bill to review visible line items, tax, service charges, included gratuity, totals, and possible duplicate-tip risks. Confirm the original bill before paying because image analysis can make mistakes.

How do I delete a saved scan?

Open Saved Scans, choose the scan you want to remove, and use the delete option. Deleting a receipt scan also removes its saved receipt image.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email vemuni@bestvaluellc.com from the address associated with your account and request deletion. We may ask you to verify ownership of the account.

What should I include in a bug report?

Include the steps you took, what you expected, what happened instead, your iPhone model, and iOS version. A screenshot is useful, but remove personal or sensitive information first.
